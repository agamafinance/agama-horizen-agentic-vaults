Private strategy vaults on Horizen

Deposit into a strategy's vault. The strategy, here two rules and an AI agent, trades it without revealing its holdings, its orders or its logic. Every three minutes a zero-knowledge proof, verified on Horizen, moves its private book, prices your shares and pays redemptions. Your deposit and your exit price are proven; nothing else is revealed. Testnet: deposits are ZENDEX's faucet tUSDC.
